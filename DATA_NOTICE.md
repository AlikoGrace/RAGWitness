# Data and third-party material

The MIT license applies to author-created software and its documentation. It does not grant rights to parliamentary publications, quoted document excerpts, retained third-party trace/data artifacts, or manuscript figure assets. Those materials remain subject to their respective rights and applicable terms.

The full Hansard corpus, source PDFs, and vector indexes are excluded. The public document manifest and exact chunk identifiers allow matching the historical snapshot; retained experiment logs include the passages actually logged and synthetic attack material necessary for evidence inspection. No blanket corpus redistribution license has been established in this review.

The supplied result JSON and logs are research evidence, not evidence of production incidents. Attack configurations contain evaluator ground truth; reconstruction uses observed artifacts and a trusted clean reference, with labels used separately for scoring. Langfuse exports are intentionally retained raw for fidelity checks; trace/project IDs are identifiers, not access credentials. No API credentials are included.
